# HoopsPass Resale Marketplace Rules

Document HP-POL-03 · Version 1.4 · Effective 15 September 2025 · Owner: Marketplace

The HoopsPass Resale marketplace lets fans resell tickets to other fans (fan-to-fan). Resale orders are recorded with order type RESALE and ticket type RSL.

## 1. Who can list

1.1 Any HoopsPass customer with a COMPLETED order can list tickets from that order, including single games from a season package.

1.2 Complimentary tickets cannot be resold. Listing a comp ticket closes the listing and may suspend the account.

## 2. Price limits

2.1 The listing price must be between 45% and 400% of the face value of the ticket. Listings outside that range are rejected.

2.2 Sellers set their own price inside the range. HoopsPass shows the face value next to every listing.

## 3. Fees

3.1 The buyer pays a 10% fee on the resale price.

3.2 The seller pays a 10% commission on the resale price, deducted from the payout.

3.3 The face value goes to nobody: the original sale to the first buyer was already counted. A resale is a new sale of the same seat and counts as a sold ticket for volume reporting, but not for sell-through.

## 4. Payouts and transfers

4.1 Sellers are paid 5 business days after the game, once the ticket has been scanned or the game has taken place.

4.2 Listings close 2 hours before tip-off. The buyer receives a new barcode; the seller's barcode is voided at the moment of sale.

## 5. Refunds

5.1 Resale purchases are not refundable, except when the game is cancelled (HP-POL-01, section 3). In that case the buyer is refunded in full, including the buyer fee, and the seller's payout is reversed.

## 6. Why resale is not sell-through

6.1 Sell-through measures how much of the HoopsPass primary allocation was sold. Resale seats are outside that allocation, so they never count toward sell-through (see HP-POL-07).
