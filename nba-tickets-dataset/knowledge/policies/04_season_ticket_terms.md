# HoopsPass Season Ticket Holder Terms

Document HP-POL-04 · Version 5.1 · Effective 4 August 2025 · Owner: Season Sales

## 1. The package

1.1 A season package covers all 41 regular-season home games of one team, in the same seats for every game. It is sold as one order with order type SEASON and 41 tickets of ticket type SSN per seat.

1.2 Season packages went on sale on 4 August 2025, six weeks before single-game tickets (15 September 2025).

1.3 The package price is 85% of the combined face value of the 41 games. A service fee of 8% of the package price applies.

## 2. Payment

2.1 Packages are paid in full by card or bank transfer, or in three instalments (August, October, December) for packages above 3,000 USD.

2.2 A missed instalment suspends the package after 14 days. Tickets for games after the suspension are voided.

## 3. Using the seats

3.1 Holders can transfer a game to a friend for free in the HoopsPass app, or resell it on the marketplace (HP-POL-03).

3.2 Holders who leave three consecutive home games unscanned and untransferred are contacted by their account manager. No penalty applies, but unused seats lower renewal priority.

## 4. Playoffs

4.1 Season ticket holders get first right to buy the same seats for home play-in and playoff games, at the playoff face value. Playoff games are not included in the package price.

4.2 The right expires 48 hours after the playoff seats are offered.

## 5. Benefits

5.1 Season ticket holders qualify for the All-Star tier of HoopsPass Rewards (HP-POL-05) at the next annual tier recalculation on 1 July, or keep a higher tier if they have one. Joining the program is the holder's choice.

## 6. Refunds

6.1 No partial refunds. Cancelled home games are credited at 1/41 of the package price (HP-POL-01, section 3.3).

## 7. Renewal

7.1 Renewal for the 2026-27 season opens on 1 May 2026 for current holders, before the general sale. Seats are held until 15 June 2026.
