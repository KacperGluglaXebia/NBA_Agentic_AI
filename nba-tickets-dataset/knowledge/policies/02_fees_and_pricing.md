# HoopsPass Fees and Pricing Guide

Document HP-POL-02 · Version 2.0 · Effective 4 August 2025 · Owner: Finance

This guide explains every amount a fan pays and what HoopsPass keeps. Finance uses the same definitions for reporting.

## 1. Who gets what

1.1 The ticket price (face value, or the discounted price) is passed through to the home team. It is not HoopsPass revenue.

1.2 HoopsPass net revenue is the service fees plus the resale commissions on sold tickets. Nothing else.

1.3 Gross merchandise value (GMV) is the total paid by fans for sold tickets: ticket price plus service fee. GMV is a volume measure and must never be reported as revenue.

1.4 A ticket counts as sold only when its order is COMPLETED and the ticket is not complimentary.

## 2. Service fees

2.1 Single-game and group tickets: 12% of the ticket price plus 1.50 USD per ticket.

2.2 Season packages: 8% of the package price, no fixed amount.

2.3 Complimentary tickets: no fee, ticket price 0.

2.4 Resale marketplace: the buyer pays a 10% fee on the resale price. See section 4 for the seller side.

## 3. Discounts

3.1 Promo codes give 5% to 15% off the face value of single-game tickets. Active codes in 2025-26: EARLYBIRD, FAMILY4, STUDENT10, REWARDS15, FLASH20 and MILITARY10. Codes cannot be combined and do not apply to group, season or resale tickets.

3.2 Group orders of 10 or more seats in one order get 20% off face value.

3.3 Season packages are priced at 85% of the face value of all 41 home games (a 15% discount).

## 4. Resale commission

4.1 When a fan resells a ticket, HoopsPass keeps a 10% seller commission on the resale price, in addition to the 10% buyer fee. On a 200 USD resale HoopsPass earns 20 USD buyer fee plus 20 USD commission.

4.2 The rest of the resale price goes to the seller. Resale tickets are seats outside the HoopsPass primary allocation.

## 5. Price tiers

5.1 Every seat belongs to one of six price tiers: Courtside (P1), Lower center (P2), Lower ends (P3), Club (P4), Upper center (P5) and Upper ends (P6).

5.2 Face values are set per game by the home team using demand-based pricing: weekend games, national-TV games, rivalry games and the playoffs are priced higher.

## 6. Average ticket price

6.1 Average ticket price (ATP) is the average price paid per sold primary ticket. It excludes complimentary and resale tickets and does not include fees.
