# HoopsPass Allocation and Complimentary Ticket Policy

Document HP-POL-07 · Version 1.0 · Effective 4 August 2025 · Owner: Partnerships

## 1. Allocation

1.1 For every game, the home team gives HoopsPass an allocation: a fixed number of seats that HoopsPass may sell (allocated_seats). The rest of the arena is sold by the team directly or by other partners.

1.2 The allocation is set per game and per price tier, before single-game tickets go on sale.

## 2. Sell-through

2.1 Sell-through rate = primary tickets sold / allocated seats, computed per game first and then summed over the games in scope.

2.2 Primary tickets are tickets inside the allocation: single-game, group and season-package tickets. Resale tickets are outside the allocation and never count. Complimentary tickets are not sold and never count.

2.3 Never divide by arena capacity. Capacity is the whole arena; HoopsPass only sells its allocation.

2.4 Target: 85% sell-through for the regular season. Teams below 75% after the first 20 home games are reviewed with the team's ticketing office.

## 3. Complimentary tickets (comps)

3.1 Comps are free tickets issued to sponsors, community programs, players' families and media. They are recorded with ticket type COMP and a ticket price of 0.

3.2 The comp budget is 1.2% of the allocation for regular-season games and 0.5% for play-in and playoff games.

3.3 Comps are not sales. They are excluded from tickets sold, GMV, net revenue, average ticket price and the no-show rate.

3.4 Comp requests above the budget need approval from the home team and the HoopsPass Head of Partnerships.
