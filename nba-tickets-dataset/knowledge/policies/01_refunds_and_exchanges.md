# HoopsPass Refund and Exchange Policy

Document HP-POL-01 · Version 3.2 · Effective 4 August 2025 · Owner: Customer Operations

HoopsPass Inc. sells NBA tickets on behalf of the home team. This policy defines when a fan can get money back, how much, and how the order is recorded. It applies to every order placed for the 2025-26 season, regular season, play-in and playoffs.

## 1. General rule

1.1 All sales are final unless one of the cases in sections 2 to 5 applies.

1.2 A refunded order is recorded with order status REFUNDED. Its tickets are voided and no longer count as sold. Refunds go back to the original payment method only.

1.3 HoopsPass service fees are non-refundable, except when the game is cancelled (section 3) or the refund is caused by a HoopsPass error (section 5).

## 2. Change of mind (single-game and group orders)

2.1 A single-game or group order can be refunded on request within 48 hours of purchase, provided the game is at least 7 days away when the request is made.

2.2 The refund covers the ticket price only. The service fee (12% of the ticket price plus 1.50 USD per ticket) is kept.

2.3 After 48 hours, or inside 7 days of the game, a change of mind is not refundable. The fan can list the tickets on the HoopsPass Resale marketplace instead (see HP-POL-03).

## 3. Cancelled games

3.1 If the NBA or the home team cancels a game without a rescheduled date, every order for that game is refunded automatically, including all HoopsPass fees.

3.2 The refund is issued within 10 business days of the cancellation announcement. No action is needed from the fan.

3.3 Season-package holders receive a credit of 1/41 of the package price per cancelled home game, applied to the next season's renewal or refunded on request.

## 4. Postponed and rescheduled games

4.1 If a game is postponed, tickets stay valid for the rescheduled date. Seats do not change.

4.2 A fan who cannot attend the new date can request a full refund of the ticket price and fees within 30 days of the announcement of the new date.

4.3 After those 30 days the tickets are treated as tickets for a normal game and section 2 applies.

## 5. HoopsPass errors

5.1 Duplicate charges, wrong seats, or tickets not delivered 24 hours before tip-off are refunded in full, fees included, with no time limit.

## 6. Resale and season tickets

6.1 Tickets bought on the Resale marketplace are not refundable, except under section 3 (cancelled games).

6.2 Season packages have no partial refunds. Unused games can be resold on the marketplace; the seller commission then applies.

## 7. Chargebacks

7.1 A payment disputed with the bank is recorded as CHARGEBACK and its tickets are voided immediately.

7.2 The customer account is suspended until the dispute is resolved. Fans should contact HoopsPass before disputing a charge; a refund eligible under this policy is always faster than a chargeback.

## 8. Cancelled before payment

8.1 Orders whose payment never settled are recorded as CANCELLED. Nothing is charged and nothing is refunded.
