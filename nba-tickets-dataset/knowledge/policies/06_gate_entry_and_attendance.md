# HoopsPass Gate Entry and Attendance Policy

Document HP-POL-06 · Version 1.1 · Effective 15 September 2025 · Owner: Venue Operations

## 1. Entry

1.1 All HoopsPass tickets are digital. Each ticket carries a single barcode that is scanned at the arena gate; the scan time is stored as scanned_at.

1.2 Gates open 90 minutes before tip-off. Barcodes become valid 6 hours before tip-off and are refreshed every 30 seconds in the HoopsPass app to prevent screenshots.

1.3 A ticket can be scanned once. A second scan is rejected and logged.

## 2. No-shows

2.1 A no-show is a sold ticket (COMPLETED order, not complimentary) that was never scanned.

2.2 The no-show rate is the share of sold tickets that were never scanned. Voided tickets (refunded, cancelled, chargeback) and complimentary tickets are not part of the calculation.

2.3 Typical no-show rates in 2025-26: about 6% for resale tickets, 7% for single-game tickets, 10% for group tickets and 14% for season-package games. Complimentary tickets have the highest unscanned share (about 25%), which is why they are excluded from the no-show rate.

## 3. Reporting attendance

3.1 HoopsPass reports scanned HoopsPass tickets only. Arena attendance (games.attendance) is the home team's official figure for the whole arena, including seats HoopsPass never sold, and must not be compared with HoopsPass ticket counts.

## 4. Denied entry

4.1 Voided tickets (from refunded, cancelled or chargeback orders, or a resold seat) are rejected at the gate. The fan is directed to the HoopsPass service desk.

4.2 HoopsPass does not refund tickets for fans refused entry under the arena code of conduct.
