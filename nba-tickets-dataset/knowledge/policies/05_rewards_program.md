# HoopsPass Rewards Program

Document HP-POL-05 · Version 2.3 · Effective 1 July 2025 · Owner: Marketing

HoopsPass Rewards is the free loyalty program for HoopsPass customers. In the data the tier is stored as loyalty_tier_code 0 to 4.

## 1. Tiers

| Code | Tier | How to qualify | Main benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | None | Not a member | - |
| 1 | Rookie | Sign up, free | Early access to flash sales, birthday offer |
| 2 | Starter | 5 or more games attended in a season | 24-hour presale, members-only giveaways |
| 3 | All-Star | 15 or more games attended, or season ticket holder | 48-hour presale, priority entry lane, free ticket transfer |
| 4 | MVP | Invitation only, top 1% of spenders | 72-hour presale, dedicated account manager, courtside experiences |

## 2. Rules

2.1 A game counts as attended when the ticket was scanned at the gate. Unscanned tickets do not count.

2.2 Tiers are recalculated on 1 July for the following season. A tier earned during the season applies from the next order.

2.3 Rewards benefits never change the ticket price or the HoopsPass service fee. Every member pays the standard fees in HP-POL-02.

2.4 The REWARDS15 promo code is sent to Starter members and above once per season.

## 3. Data and privacy

3.1 Membership and tier history are used for marketing only with the customer's opt-in (marketing_opt_in).

3.2 Reports on members are aggregated. Individual members are identified only by customer_id in analytics (see HP-POL-08).
